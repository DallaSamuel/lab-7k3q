Privacy note

PipFrame test version. Last updated 26 October 2026.

The short version

Your trades, prices, balances, notes and plans stay on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you say yes to joining the test. If you do, the app sends usage numbers that are not linked to your name, they are kept while the test runs, and deleted when you ask or when the test ends, and you can stop at any time.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type: trades, prices, lot sizes, balances, equity, profit and loss, notes, reasons, checklists, plans, moods and prop firm settings. It lives in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser's data deletes it, so back it up from Settings. A backup is a file you keep. Give it a password and it is locked (encrypted), so nobody can read it without the password.

What is sent if you join the test

Only if you tap "Yes, join the test" or switch on sharing in Settings:

  • A random ID made on your phone (16 letters and numbers), not your name or number
  • App version, the day, and how many days you have used it
  • Phone type and model where the browser shows it (Android does, iPhone does not), browser, screen size, language, and whether the app is installed
  • Your time zone, your local time and the matching US Central time
  • How long you used the app, how many visits, and which screens and buttons you used, with the time
  • Counts only: calculations, trades logged, exits entered, wins, losses, graded trades, mistakes the app caught (for example a stop loss on the wrong side), imports, backups
  • Anything you choose to type in the feedback box and send

It is not linked to your name, but because a random ID and your phone details are kept together, data protection law (such as Nigeria's NDPA and Europe's GDPR) treats it as personal data. We treat it that way too.

What is never sent

Prices, balances, equity, profit or loss amounts, lot sizes, instruments you trade, notes, reasons, plans, checklist wording, your name, your contacts, your exact location, or your backup files. The app does not store your internet address. Google, which runs the spreadsheet the numbers go to, sees the internet address of any request when it arrives, as every website does.

Where it goes and who sees it

A private Google Sheet run by the PipFrame test team (CyberJKD). Only the team can open it. It is used to see which parts of the app help and which confuse people. It is never sold, never used for ads, and never shared outside the team.

How long it is kept

For as long as the test runs, so the team can compare how the app is used over months, not just days. When the test ends, the usage data is deleted. If you ask, your rows are deleted sooner (see Your choices).

Your choices

Switch sharing off any time in Settings, under Help improve PipFrame. That also deletes the activity waiting on your phone. To have what was already sent deleted, or to see it, give your ID (shown in the same place, the line starting "id") to the person who invited you to the test. It will be deleted.

Not advice

PipFrame is a calculator and journal. It does not give financial advice or trading signals. You are responsible for your own trading decisions and risk. The test is for adults.
