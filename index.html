Help test PipFrame

If you say yes, the app quietly shares how you use it, so the people building it can see what works and what confuses you. No sign-up and it works in the background.

Shared: your phone type and time zone, when and how long you use it, which buttons you press, the mistakes the app catches (like a stop loss on the wrong side), and how many trades you win, lose and grade.
Never shared: prices, balances, profit or loss amounts, lot sizes, notes, reasons, names, your exact location or your internet address.

You can switch it off any time in Settings, and that deletes what was waiting to be sent.

How to test

  1. Size a trade: enter a balance, risk, entry and stop loss, then tap Calculate.
  2. Log it, then open the Journal and add an exit price.
  3. Fix a mistake with Edit, and try Undo after a delete.
  4. Add demo trades above and open Review.
  5. Install the app, turn on airplane mode, and open it again.
  6. Tell us anything confusing, missing or wrong in Help us test below.
  7. Open the Prop tab, enter your firm's rules, then tick your rules for the day.

PipFrame is a calculation tool, not advice. Please do not enter anything you would not want on your own device.

Help improve PipFrame

Off by default. If you switch it on, the app sends a small summary when you open it (at most a few times a day): your phone type and time zone, the time on your clock, how long you actively used the app, simple counts, and a list of which screens and buttons you used with the time. It never sends what you type or see: no prices, balances, profit or loss, lot sizes, notes, reasons or names. It does not send your exact location or your internet address. Switch it off any time.

About, privacy and your data

Everything you enter stays on this device, in this browser. There is no account and no server copy of your journal.

Back up often from Settings, because clearing browser data deletes the journal. To remove everything, use Delete all trades in Settings.

Shared cards are made on your device and only go where you send them.

If you join the test, the app also sends anonymous usage numbers while you use it. You can read exactly what is sent in Settings under Help improve PipFrame, and switch it off any time. It never sends your trades, prices, balances or notes.
